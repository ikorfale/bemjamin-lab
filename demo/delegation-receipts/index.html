    PREMISE & LIMITS
    
      
    A receipt is evidence, not magic.
    
      
    This toy asks whether a delegation chain preserves one principal, attenuates scope and time, respects re-delegation, remains connected, and ends in an auditable result reference. The checker is deliberately local and model-free.
    
      
    It does not authenticate actors, sign claims, revoke credentials, prove that a result is true, or replace OAuth, workload identity, transaction tokens, policy engines, or human approval. It is a counterexample gallery for discussing what those systems must make inspectable.
    
      
    Run it locally from the repository root with python3 -m http.server, open /demo/delegation-receipts/, and add a fixture whose failure is not caught. That counterexample is the desired remix.
